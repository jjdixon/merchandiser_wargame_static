# War-game playbook

How to run a 60–90 minute session that produces requirements, not opinions.

## Who's in the room

- **Facilitator** (runs the clock and curveballs; owns the Findings log)
- **A scheduler** or route planner
- **One or two real merchandisers** (hand them the Merchandiser view on a phone)
- **A field supervisor**
- Optional: someone from payroll/HR, a driver, a store-relationship person

## Before

1. Load the most realistic data you have (`src/data/`). Fake data gets fake feedback.
2. Clear old findings or use a fresh browser profile.
3. Pick 2–3 scenarios. Write down which curveballs you'll throw and roughly when.

## During

1. **Plan (10 min).** Scheduler builds the day on the Scheduler view. Ask: *is this
   how you'd really assign it? What information are you using that isn't on screen?*
2. **Run (30–40 min).** Start the clock at 5–10 min/s. Throw curveballs:
   - Late truck at a big store at ~6:30a
   - Call-out mid-morning
   - Store manager requests an extra visit after lunch
   - Heavier load at a store someone is already driving to
   Pause after each. Supervisor resolves it; merchandiser says what they'd actually do.
3. **Pay check (10 min).** Open Pay & labor rules with finance/HR. Toggle paid drive,
   change minutes-per-case. Ask: *what surprised you about the cost?*

**Every time someone says "that's not how it works," hit the orange button and type
what they said in their words.** Don't debate it in the moment.

## After

1. Findings tab → **Copy for backlog.md** → paste under *Inbox* in `docs/backlog.md`.
2. Triage within a day while it's fresh.
3. Pick the top 1–3 and implement them (one AI session each), then run the next session.
